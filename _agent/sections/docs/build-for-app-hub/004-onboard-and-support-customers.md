## Article introduction

[Webex App Hub](https://apphub.webex.com/) helps Webex customers discover apps that improve their workflows. List your app to showcase its benefits, explain its requirements, and give customers a clear way to get started.

## Selected section

## Onboard and support customers

Use [Plan Customer Setup and Authorization](https://developer.webex.com/app-hub/docs/app-hub-customer-onboarding) to separate your preparation from customer approval and configuration. Continue with [Manage Your Published App](https://developer.webex.com/app-hub/docs/manage-your-published-app) for updates, support, ownership changes, and offboarding. Check the [API and SDK Support Policy](https://developer.webex.com/app-hub/docs/webex-api-and-sdk-support-policy) when planning dependency updates.



> **Installing or approving an app?**
>
> If you are a customer administrator, start with the developer's setup instructions and the app-specific guidance linked from [customer setup and authorization](https://developer.webex.com/app-hub/docs/app-hub-customer-onboarding). For an agentic app, use the [Admin Approval Guide](https://developer.webex.com/app-hub/docs/admin-approval-guide-for-agentic-apps).
