## Article introduction

[Webex App Hub](https://apphub.webex.com/) helps Webex customers discover apps that improve their workflows. List your app to showcase its benefits, explain its requirements, and give customers a clear way to get started.

## Selected section

## Publish your app

Follow [Submission and review](https://developer.webex.com/app-hub/docs/app-hub-submission-process) to prepare listing details, support and privacy information, and access for testing. Registration, App Hub publication, and customer authorization are separate steps.

Use the additional requirements that match your app:

- [Embedded Apps submission checklist](https://developer.webex.com/app-hub/docs/app-hub-submission-checklist-for-embedded-apps).
- [Agentic Apps submission and badging](https://developer.webex.com/app-hub/docs/agentic-apps-app-hub).
