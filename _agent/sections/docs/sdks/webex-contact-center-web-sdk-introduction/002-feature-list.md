## Article introduction

Enhance your applications with the Webex Contact Center SDK and redefine customer interactions with powerful, scalable, and intelligent contact center solutions.

---

With the Webex Contact Center SDK, you can seamlessly integrate essential contact center functionalities into your applications, empowering agents with robust capabilities. The SDK enables `agent registration`, `station login`, `state management`, `call handling`, and `task management`, allowing you to build a comprehensive and efficient customer support experience. ## Feature List

The Webex Contact Center SDK offers a wide range of features to enhance your contact center applications:

- **Station Login**: Allow agents to login to their stations.
- **State Management**: Manage `agent states` such as available, idle, and busy.
- **Task Management**: Manage an agent's tasks or interactions.
- **Consult and Transfer**: Allow agents to consult and transfer tasks.
- **Recording Control**: Pause and resume call recordings.
- **Buddy Agents**: Fetch and display a list of buddy agents.
- **OutDial**: Initiate outbound calls from the contact center.
- **Real Time Assist**: Request and receive AI-generated suggested responses for an active task.
