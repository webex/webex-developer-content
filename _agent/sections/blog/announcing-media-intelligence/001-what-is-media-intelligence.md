## What is Media Intelligence?

Media Intelligence is a developer-focused Webex offer that extends Webex media to third-party applications. It provides APIs that let developers connect their applications to real-time media, process it with their preferred services, and deliver the results to their customers’ systems and workflows.

Media Intelligence gives you the flexibility to choose how media is processed, which services you integrate, how insights appear in your product, and which workflows they trigger.

With Media Intelligence, you can:

- Extend Webex meeting media into AI, analytics, workflow, and industry-specific applications.
- Build differentiated capabilities for specific customers, industries, and business processes.
- Turn live media into timely product features, insights, and workflow actions.

Real Time Media Streaming is the first API in Media Intelligence. We plan to introduce transcription, translation, and sentiment analysis APIs in the future, giving you more ways to use Webex capabilities directly in your applications.
