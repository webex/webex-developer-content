The Webex iOS Messaging SDK empowers you to integrate Webex Messaging capabilities into your mobile applications seamlessly.

---



> **Info**
>

The SDK provides a comprehensive suite of methods enabling the creation and management of spaces and teams, sending and receiving messages, and even supports enriched messaging with attachments, @mentions, and message read receipts.
