Welcome to the June edition of the Webex for Developers newsletter, highlighting all that’s going on with the Webex platform, community, and ecosystem. 

### 🎤 Webex for Developers at Cisco Live US – 2022 🎤

The Webex team has been laser-focused on [Cisco Live US in Las Vegas](https://www.ciscolive.com/global.html), taking place this month. Cisco DevNet and the Webex Developer Evangelism team have teamed up to provide a [comprehensive showcase of the Webex Developer platform](https://developer.webex.com/blog/join-webex-developers-at-cisco-live-us-2022-in-vegas). 

### 💻 Webex Apps Hackathon Results 💻

Check out the Webex Community forums and see the results of last month’s [Webex Apps Hackathon](https://community.cisco.com/t5/webex-for-developers/hackathon-submission-thread/m-p/4596440/thread-id/688). Congratulations to the winning teams!

### 🆕 Webex Meetings Simultaneous Interpretation API 🆕

Come see how developers can [leverage Webex Meetings Simultaneous Interpretation API](https://developer.webex.com/blog/webex-meetings-simultaneous-interpretation-api-update) for assigning live language interpreters to unlimited meeting audio channels.

### 📱Webex Mobile SDKs v3.5 Update📱

Check out the changelog for v3.5 of the Webex [iOS SDK](https://github.com/webex/webex-ios-sdk/blob/3.5.0/CHANGELOG.md#350) and [Android SDK ](https://github.com/webex/webex-android-sdk/blob/3.5.0_G.A/CHANGELOG.md#350), packed with new features and enhancements.

### 🚨Keep Up to Date with Webex API Changelog🚨
