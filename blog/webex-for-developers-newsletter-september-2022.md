### 🆕 Developer Platform Updates🆕

As usual, the Webex Developer platform has more new updates to learn more about this month. First, Webex Developer Evangelist, Aaron Burns, walks us through some great [New Features in the Webex Meetings API](https://developer.webex.com/blog/3-new-features-in-the-webex-meetings-api):

- [Closed captions](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/meeting-closed-captions) (also called meeting transcripts), are now retrievable from the API without needing to record the meeting itself.
- The creation and management of [break-out sessions](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/nroo6fs/Breakout-sessions-in-Webex-Meetings-and-Webex-Webinars) is now available.
- Administrators can now [retrieve complete meeting data](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/meetings/list-meetings) for a user, even if they belong to an organization with multiple sites, with the new `siteURL` parameter.


In addition to the Meetings API updates, Cisco Product Manager for Identity Management & Security, Nick Wooler, introduces another new feature: [Login with Webex on Developer Applications and 3rd Party Platforms](https://developer.webex.com/blog/login-with-webex-on-developer-applications-and-3rd-party-platforms). This was a common feature request, and we are excited to announce its availability. One of the more noteworthy aspects of *Login with Webex* is that it allows devices without a keyboard, such as an automobile, to authenticate on the Webex platform, making it a frictionless user experience.
