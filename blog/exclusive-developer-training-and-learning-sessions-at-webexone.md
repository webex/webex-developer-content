We’re excited to announce new, interactive training for WebexOne 2022. Our community of developers plays a critical part in guiding the future of the collaboration experience. That’s why we’re making it more intuitive to build in Webex—and why we’re launching live technical training to keep you up to date!
Before we dive into technical training, let’s highlight a few higher-level learning sessions you might find insightful

### Learning sessions for developers

WebexOne is a two-day virtual event featuring a wealth of insights from global luminaries, technical gurus, and industry thought leaders. It all kicks off on October 25<sup>th</sup>! You won’t want to miss the opening keynote, and there are some exciting learning sessions for developers too.

#### Recommended Session #1: October 25<sup>th</sup>

[Hybrid tools tailored for every workstyle.](https://www.webexone.com/agenda.html?utm_medium=website&utm_source=wdcblog&utm_campaign=2022_wx1_developer&utm_channel=developer&utm_content=developer_blog&team=developer#P003)

This session will dive deep into the Webex platform and explore the power of Webex apps and integrations to simplify every workflow. Plus, you’ll hear from organizations who’ve built incredibly personalized experiences with Webex APIs, bots, widgets, and SDKs

#### Recommended Session #2 – October 26<sup>th</sup>

[Your customers want choices. We can help.](https://www.webexone.com/agenda.html?utm_medium=website&utm_source=wdcblog&utm_campaign=2022_wx1_developer&utm_channel=developer&utm_content=developer_blog&team=developer#CP005)

This session will cover how CPaaS (Communications Platform as a Service) can help companies create automated customer journeys to deliver rich, personalized experience. We’ll delve into cost efficiencies, real-time integration capabilities, low-code tools, and programmable APIs that enable CPaaS users to create personalized, automated interactions for customers.

#### Recommended Session #3 – October 25<sup>th</sup>  

[Enabling Seamless hybrid work with Webex + Meraki + ThousandEyes.](https://www.webexone.com/agenda.html?utm_medium=website&utm_source=wdcblog&utm_campaign=2022_wx1_developer&utm_channel=developer&utm_content=developer_blog&team=developer#CS004)  

We’ll take you through a core solution for hybrid work: Webex, Meraki, and ThousandEyes. With the power of this integration, you can troubleshoot hundreds (or thousands) of sites at a moment’s notice, enable best-in-class hybrid collaboration and ensure industry-leading security and performance.

#### Recommended Session #4 – October 25<sup>th</sup>

[Harness the power of Webex Calling and Meetings by integrating with our modern APIs.](https://www.webexone.com/agenda.html?utm_medium=website&utm_source=wdcblog&utm_campaign=2022_wx1_developer&utm_channel=developer&utm_content=developer_blog&team=developer#AS004)

Learn how Webex APIs give you easy access to the Webex Platform. We’ll dive into how you can build powerful Integrations that automate the Webex Calling and Meetings experience and strengthen Webex adoption across your company.  
WebexOne will feature a range of learning sessions that cover hybrid work, customer experience and everything from our platform to CPaaS. 

Take a moment to browse our entire two-day [agenda of sessions here.](https://www.webexone.com/agenda.html?utm_medium=website&utm_source=wdcblog&utm_campaign=2022_wx1_developer&utm_channel=developer&utm_content=developer_blog&team=developer) Next, let’s spotlight some technical training sessions for developers.

### Technical training for developers

For the first time, WebexOne will offer live, interactive\* training for developers. This is an opportunity to broaden your skillset with technical experts and Webex instructors.  

[Introduction to the Webex Developer platform. October 25th 1pm – 1:50pm PT](https://www.webexone.com/product-training.html?utm_medium=website&utm_source=wdcblog&utm_campaign=2022_wx1_developer&utm_channel=developer&utm_content=developer_blog&team=developer#PT002) 

This course will cover all the fundamentals about the Webex Platform. You’ll learn how to integrate Webex into your existing hybrid workflows and more.

[Building Webex Embedded Apps. October 25th 2pm – 2:50pm PT](https://www.webexone.com/product-training.html?utm_medium=website&utm_source=wdcblog&utm_campaign=2022_wx1_developer&utm_channel=developer&utm_content=developer_blog&team=developer#PT003) 

Discover the possibilities of Webex Embedded Apps for Meetings and Spaces. You’ll get a full overview of Embedded Apps, tips, and strategies for administering apps, and learn how you can build your own!
