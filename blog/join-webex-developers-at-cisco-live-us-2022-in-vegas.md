> Official Webex documentation: [View this page on Webex for Developers](https://developer.webex.com/blog/join-webex-developers-at-cisco-live-us-2022-in-vegas)

This year, at Cisco Live US in Las Vegas, Cisco DevNet and Webex for Developers have teamed up to provide a comprehensive showcase of all things Webex! Every day the DevNet Zone is open and attendees will be able to learn about how the Webex platform enables developers to power the hybrid experience.

### Webex Wednesday Takeover

On Wednesday, June 15th, Webex will be taking over the DevNet Zone! There will be special Webex focused activities, drinks and snacks, and even some giveaways throughout the day. Be sure to attend the capstone talk (DEVNET-1605) at 4:00 PM in the DevNet Theater to see how Webex enables developers to power the hybrid experience.

### Other DevNet Zone Activities

Come listen-in and learn at the speaker sessions, get your hands dirty building actual applications in the Webex workshops, and drop by the Webex booth to chat with Webex engineers, evangelists, and product managers.

### Speaker Sessions

Here are all the Webex speaker sessions scheduled for Cisco Live:

- [DEVNET-1850: Introducing Webex Connect and CPaaS APIs](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVNET-1850#/)
  - Host: David Staudt
  - Date: Monday, June 13th, 12:00 PM
  - Webex Connect & CPaaS technologies explore the automation & integration possibilities offered by the APIs and SDKs included. Webex Connect/CPaaS provide connectivity, integration and tools so cloud CRM can engage with customers via preferred channels.
- [DEVNET-2046: Making "The Possible" a Reality with Collaboration Programmability](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVNET-2046#/)
  - Host: Nate Bartelings
  - Date: Monday, June 13th, 4:00 PM
  - In this session participants will learn about the available programmability options available in Cisco's Collaboration stack, including APIs, Widgets, BOTS and available integrations. We will discuss real life applications and use cases of programmability within the business. Participants will be encouraged to open their minds to the "Art of the Possible" and think outside of what is provided in our "out of the box" products to an ideal solution. This solution uses programmability and the openness of our platform to allow us to re-imagine how we solve broken workflows and build a bridge to connect users or simplify the collaboration experience.
- [DEVNET-2100: Automating Migration to Webex Calling](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVNET-2100#/)
  - Host: Scott Anderson
  - Date: Tuesday June 14th, 2:00 PM
  - Webex Calling has become radically more flexible and easier to migrate with the addition of hundreds of new APIs and bulk provisioning capabilities. In this session you’ll see a brief demo illustrating options available to export data from our legacy call control platform, Cisco Unified Communications Manager, and drill down into the breath of new Webex Calling APIs and bulk provisioning tools with real-world examples. Attendees will also have an opportunity to ask the product owner of these new tools and APIs questions on what’s available, what are the limitations, and what is on the horizon.
- [DEVNET-2260: Webex API "What's New" - The latest / greatest new features in Webex app development](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVNET-2260#/)
  - Host: Phil Bellanti
  - Date: Tuesday June 14th, 4:00 PM
  - Learn about the newest Webex API releases and other developer updates and how they can be leveraged to power some exciting collaboration use cases.
- [DEVNET-1605: Powering Hybrid experiences with the Webex Platform](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVNET-1605#/)
  - Host: Phil Bellanti/Kedar Ganta
  - Date: Wednesday June 15th 4:00 PM
  - In the new hybrid world, organizations across various industries are increasingly looking for ways to seamlessly collaborate while using different applications for their business needs. The Webex platform is powering those seamless experiences while enabling speed, agility, and resilience for these organizations. We will showcase a few hero use cases and explore developer tools and resources to help you build these experiences. Come learn how different partners are enabling these experiences using the Webex platform.

### Workshops

Workshops are smaller sessions that allow you to get hands-on building within the Webex ecosystem.

- [DEVWKS-1940: Assistance Needed: Develop Skills for the Webex Assistant](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVWKS-1940#/)
  - Host: David Liedle
  - Date: Monday June 13th, 2:00 PM
  - Learn about the new features within Webex Assistant skills. These skills give Webex devices a whole new level of power! This session will cover how to use skills and how to build your own.
- [DEVWKS-2262: Webex Connect and CPaaS Workshop](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVWKS-2262#/)
  - Host: David Staudt
  - Date: Tuesday June 14th, 2:00 PM
  - In this hands-on DevNet workshop session, dive into an instructor-led, step-by-step tutorial on building automation and integration capabilities with the new Webex Connect/CPaaS technologies.
- [DEVWKS-2429: Automating workflows with Webex Adaptive Cards](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVWKS-2429#/)
  - Host: Oleksii Borisenko
  - Date: Tuesday June 14th, 3:00 PM
  - In this hands-on workshop, attendees will learn how to create Adaptive Cards in Webex and extend their functionality to manage responsive workflows with network infrastructure.
- [DEVWKS-1650: Building your first Embedded App on Webex](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/sessions/session-catalog.html?search=DEVWKS-1650#/)
  - Host: David Liedle
  - Date: Thursday June 16th, 10:00 AM
  - Let's roll up our sleeves and build a Webex Embedded App! Participants will step through the creation of an app, development, testing, and app approval requirements for submission to the App Hub.
