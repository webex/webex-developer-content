Welcome to the sizzling hot 🔥 July edition 🔥 of the Webex for Developers newsletter, highlighting all that’s going on with the Webex platform, community, and ecosystem.

### 🎤 Cisco Live US 2022 – Webex for Developer Wrap-up 🎤

The Webex for Developers team had an amazing time last month at&#xa0; [Cisco Live US in Las Vegas,](https://www.ciscolive.com/global.html) where the Cisco DevNet and Webex for Developers teams joined forces to showcase some exciting things happening with Webex and the developer community. Inside the [DevNet Zone](https://www.ciscolive.com/global/attend/education/technology-tracks/devnet.html#:~:text=What%20is%20the%20DevNet%20Zone,Developer%20Dash%2C%20and%20much%20more!), the Webex team hosted hands-on workshops, live demos of integrations, and speaking sessions featuring some wonderful Webex partners. 

Webex Developer Evangelist, Joe Zanini, summed it all up in this [great wrap-up blog](https://developer.webex.com/blog/webex-for-developers-at-cisco-live-2022).

If you weren’t able to attend all of [our developer sessions at Cisco Live](https://developer.webex.com/blog/join-webex-developers-at-cisco-live-us-2022-in-vegas) or want to see a replay, just search by the session number in the [On-Demand Library](https://www.ciscolive.com/on-demand/on-demand-library.html?search.event=1654953906132001zSK6&amp;zid=global#/) to watch the recording.
